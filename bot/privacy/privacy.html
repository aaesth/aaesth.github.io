Privacy Policy

Effective Date: August 12, 2026 | Application: Anime Conquest

1. Introduction

Anime Conquest ("we", "our", or "the Bot") is a Discord application designed to automate Roblox group rank management, role verification, and server utility for our community. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting the data collected through your use of the Bot.

2. Information We Collect

To function properly, Anime Conquest processes and stores a minimal amount of data:

  • Discord User IDs: Collected to link your Discord account with your Roblox account and maintain administrative logs regarding claimed group ranks.
  • Roblox User IDs and Usernames: Collected when you run rank verification commands (/get rank) to verify group membership and assign appropriate group roles.
  • Server Role Data: Read transiently during command execution to verify if a user holds the required Discord role (e.g., Early Access Basic, Early Access Plus, OG) to claim a group rank.

3. How We Use Your Information

The data collected is used strictly for the following purposes:

  • Verifying Discord server roles against Roblox group requirements.
  • Assigning the corresponding Roblox group rank to your account.
  • Enforcing single-claim policies so users cannot repeatedly claim or double-claim restricted group ranks.
  • Providing server administrators with log audit functionality (r$logcheck).

4. Message Content & Data Processing

  • Transient Processing: The Bot reads message text transiently only to execute administrative prefix commands (e.g., r$set, r$logcheck).
  • No Storage: Message contents are never stored, logged, or tracked off-platform.
  • No AI Training: Message content and user data are never used to train machine learning or artificial intelligence models.

5. Data Storage and Retention

Stored data (Discord IDs mapped to claimed rank types) is retained locally in restricted-access database files strictly for administrative verification and anti-abuse enforcement. We do not sell, trade, or share user data with any third parties outside of necessary interactions with the official Discord and Roblox APIs to carry out rank updates.

6. Data Removal & User Rights

If you wish to have your stored claim records removed from our database, you may submit a request by opening a support ticket in our Discord server or contacting a server administrator. Upon request, your stored ID logs will be deleted via our administrative clearing utilities (r$clearlogs).

7. Contact Us

If you have any questions or concerns regarding this Privacy Policy or the operation of Anime Conquest, please reach out via a support ticket in our official Discord server, or send an email to ow4z@proton.me
